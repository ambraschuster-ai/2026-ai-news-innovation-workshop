Revisit & Build

You've spent the day learning how to prompt, configure, and plan more deliberately. Now bring that back to your project.

2:00 PM·180 MIN·Hands-on
01

State Your Goal, Then Plan

This is where all the tools we learned throughout the day come together.

  • Were you able to update your CLAUDE.md file and understand the changes?
  • Did you setup a spec and its corresponding support files, and is Claude using them as intended?

Switch to Plan mode. State what you want to do next, in plain language. Read the plan before you approve it, specific to your project, or generic? Send it back if it's not right yet.

Notice if you explain the same thing to Claude twice. That's usually worth turning into a Skill, or an update to CLAUDE.md.

02

Build

Once you're confident in your plan with a clear set of tasks, get to building. Instructors will circulate.

🛠️ Build Time

Description: Build from the plan you just reviewed. Update your CLAUDE.md if needed, write or revise a spec, then build.

Deliverable: Visible progress on your project, checked against the list below.

  • A CLAUDE.md file, reviewed and up to date
  • At least one Skill
  • requirements.md or specifications.md
  • architecture.md
  • tasks.md
  • A working mockup or prototype of your product

If you're not sure where to start

  • Pick one small piece rather than redoing everything.
  • Ask Claude: "given what I want to accomplish, what are the next steps I could take?"
