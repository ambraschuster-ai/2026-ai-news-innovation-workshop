Day 3: Polish & Shipping

The last day is about making what you built more solid, more responsible, and more useful to someone besides you. You'll cover responsible AI design and human-AI collaboration, get an extra block of hands-on build time, and see where these skills actually show up in newsrooms, then spend the afternoon finishing your project and showing it off.

Learning Outcomes

  • Build a working sense of what "safe and auditable" AI design actually requires in practice, including how you disclose AI use to the people reading it
  • Reason through when to automate fully versus keep a human in the loop
  • Identify specific roles and functions in journalism where the skills from this workshop are already being used
  • Finish and present a working version of your project
