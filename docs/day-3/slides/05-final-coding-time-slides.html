01

What to Prioritize

75 minutes isn't much. Make what you have actually work end to end, don't add new features.

  • Would a stranger understand what this does quickly after they start using it?
  • Is there a broken feature you've been avoiding? Fix that first.
  • Rough edges or disclosure gaps from Safe & Auditable Design? Automation or human-in-the-loop decisions from Human-AI Design? Does your Build Time decision still hold up? Flag them, transparency goes a long way for the demo.
