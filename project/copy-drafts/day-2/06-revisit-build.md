# Revisit & Build

> You've spent the day learning how to prompt, configure, and plan more deliberately. Now bring that back to the project you started yesterday.

## 1. State Your Goal, Then Plan

This is where all the tools we learned throughout the day come together.

Before you touch any code, check whether the pieces from today make sense:

- Were you able to update your CLAUDE.md file and understand the changes?
- Did you setup a spec and its corresponding support files, and is Claude using them as intended?

If the answer to these is yes, then let's switch to Plan mode. We are going to state what we want to do next in plain language, the way we'd explain it to an informed colleague, and let Claude research and propose an approach. 

Read the plan before you approve it. Is it specific to your actual project, or generic enough to apply to anything? Send it back and ask Claude to keep planning if it's not right yet. 

Along the way, notice if you're explaining the same thing to Claude more than once, today or in a project you'll come back to. That's usually a sign it's worth turning into a Skill instead of just fixing it in the moment or maybe an update that needs to be made to CLAUDE.md 

Ask Claude about it directly, or note it and come back to it once you're building.

## 2. Build

Once you are confident in your plan and you have a clear set of tasks, get to building. Your instructors will circulate and be available just in case you have any questions.

> **Exercise: Build Time**
> - Time: The rest of this session.
> - Description: Build from the plan you just reviewed. Update your CLAUDE.md if it needs it, write or revise a spec for what you're building next, then build.
> - Deliverable: Visible progress on your project, checked against the list below. Something to show and talk about next.
>   - A `CLAUDE.md` file, reviewed and up to date
>   - At least one Skill
>   - `requirements.md` or `specifications.md`
>   - `architecture.md`
>   - `tasks.md`
>   - A working mockup or prototype of your product

### If you're not sure where to start

- Pick one small piece rather than redoing everything from scratch.
- Ask Claude directly: "given what I want to accomplish, what are the next steps that I could take to develop the idea?"
